Grand Hotel La Cocumella

Via Cocumella 7 · Sant'Agnello · Sorrento

One of the most storied hotels on the Sorrentine peninsula, La Cocumella has been welcoming guests since 1699 — making it one of the oldest hotels in all of Italy. Set in a former Jesuit convent surrounded by lemon groves and gardens, with private sea access, a spectacular pool and some of the finest views of the Bay of Naples you will ever encounter.

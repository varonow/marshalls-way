⚓ Life Aboard
Dress Code
Dress however you feel most comfortable — no formal dress code onboard.
House Rules
The crew will brief guests upon boarding. Please treat all spaces with care and respect.
Laundry
Laundry service is available onboard. For delicate or high-value garments, the crew uses a professional express laundry with 12-hour turnaround.
Phone Charging
US plugs (Type A) are available in all cabins. Adapters are also onboard. If anyone uses a medical device requiring power, please notify Captain Mauro in advance.
Dietary
Fish, cheese and eggs only. No meat, poultry, pork or crustaceans. The kitchen has been koshered and new pots and pans have been purchased. Chef has been fully briefed.

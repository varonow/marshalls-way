Capri
Tuesday July 7 · Via Camerelle & Via Vittorio Emanuele
Capri is the most glamorous shopping stop on the trip. Via Camerelle is the luxury boulevard — Hermès, Gucci, Prada all have boutiques here. Via Vittorio Emanuele is more artisanal and fun. Allow at least 2 hours to browse properly!

Positano
Wednesday Jul 8 & Saturday Jul 11 · Via dei Mulini
Positano is the fashion capital of the Amalfi Coast — known worldwide for its colorful moda Positanese: the distinctive handmade clothing in vibrant fabrics that has been worn by Audrey Hepburn, Jackie Kennedy and every stylish woman since. The main shopping street is Via dei Mulini leading down to the beach.
👗
Moda Positanese
The distinctive Positano style — flowing dresses, palazzo pants, kaftans and beachwear in hand-blocked prints and vibrant colors. Every item is handmade locally. Buy something to wear on the yacht!

The most famous clothing boutique in Positano — gorgeous linen dresses, palazzo pants and resort wear in beautiful Mediterranean prints. Via Pasitea 72.

Amalfi
Thursday July 9 · Piazza del Duomo & Via Lorenzo d'Amalfi
Amalfi is famous for two things above all others — its handmade paper (carta amalfitana) which has been made here since the 13th century, and its ceramics. The main shopping street fans out from the magnificent Duomo square. You'll also find excellent limoncello and local food products here.
📜
Handmade Paper
Amalfi's handmade paper is one of the great crafts of Southern Italy — used by popes and merchants for centuries. The Museo della Carta tells the story. Beautiful journals, stationery and art paper make exceptional gifts.
La Scuderia del Duca
The finest handmade paper shop in Amalfi — exquisite stationery, journals, photo albums and art paper made using 13th century methods. Piazza del Duomo.
€€
Museo della Carta
The paper museum in an ancient mill — buy beautiful handmade paper products directly from the source. Via delle Cartiere 23.

Ceramics

Ceramiche Artistiche Solimene
The most famous ceramics producer on the entire coast — based in Vietri sul Mare (which you'll visit on July 10!). The Amalfi shop has a beautiful selection.

The sfusato amalfitano — the giant Amalfi lemon — is the most prized citrus in Italy. Everything made from it here is extraordinary.
Antichi Sapori d'Amalfi

Ravello
Thursday July 9 🎂 · Marshall's Birthday · Piazza Duomo
Ravello sits high above the coast with extraordinary views — it's quieter and more refined than the coastal towns, attracting artists and musicians for centuries. Shopping here is more artisanal and unique — you're less likely to find the same things everywhere else. The tiny streets around the Duomo are worth exploring.
🏺
Ceramics & Art
Ravello has some of the finest ceramics and artisan shops on the entire coast — quality tends to be higher and crowds lower than in the coastal towns.
Ceramiche d'Arte Carmela
Exquisite hand-painted ceramics in Ravello's refined style — more sophisticated than the typical coast souvenir. Beautiful pieces for the home.
€€€
Profumi di Ravello
A tiny perfume laboratory creating fragrances inspired by the Ravello landscape — utterly unique gifts that you simply cannot find anywhere else.
€€€

Books & Antiques
Ravello has charming antique and book shops that reflect the town's long history as a cultural hub — DH Lawrence, Virginia Woolf and Gore Vidal all lived or stayed here.
Antique shops around Piazza Duomo
Several small antique and curiosity shops around the main square sell everything from old prints of the coast to vintage jewellery and local artifacts.

Sorrento
Jul 3–5 pre-voyage · Corso Italia & Via San Cesareo
Sorrento is the most accessible and least frenetic shopping destination on the trip — you have two full days here before boarding. The pedestrian Corso Italia is lined with shops, but the real character is in the narrow Via San Cesareo and the lanes of the old town, where you'll find woodworkers, leather craftsmen and food producers.
🪵
Intarsia Woodwork
Sorrento is world famous for intarsia — intricate marquetry woodwork using dozens of different wood types inlaid together. Unique to this area and absolutely beautiful. Music boxes, trays, frames and furniture.
Gargiulo & Jannuzzi
The most famous intarsia workshop in Sorrento since 1863 — stunning marquetry music boxes, trays, frames and furniture. You can watch the craftsmen working. Vico I Fuoro 1.

Coral Jewelry
The Campania coast is one of the world's great coral jewelry traditions — red and pink coral pieces, cameos carved from conch shells, and coral set in gold. Sorrento and Naples are the best places to buy.
Coral jewelry shops on Corso Italia
Several reputable coral and cameo jewelers along the main street — look for pieces with certificates of authenticity. Beautiful gifts to bring home.
